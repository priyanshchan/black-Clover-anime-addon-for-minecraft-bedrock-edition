## v1.0.0 – the full release

**Black Clover: Grimoire Awakening** is an unofficial Black Clover fan addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Receive your own grimoire at the altar, master one of **40 magic attributes**, eat an **Abyss Fruit**, lead a squad of **Magic Knight companions**, forge **armour**, and live in a **world that moves on its own** - its factions rise and fall, and things happen out there whether you go or not.

v1.0.0 is the first full release. It contains everything built since v0.1.0 (the 0.2 to 0.9 builds were never published on their own), and its headline is **a living world**.

### Download and install

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| **`BlackClover_GrimoireAwakening_v1.0.0.mcaddon`** | **Start here.** Open it and Minecraft imports both packs. |
| `BlackClover_BP_v1.0.0.mcpack` | the behavior pack on its own |
| `BlackClover_RP_v1.0.0.mcpack` | the resource pack on its own |

* Needs **Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer**. No experimental toggles.
* In your world's settings, turn on the **Black Clover** behavior pack (the resource pack comes with it).
* **Upgrading from v0.1.0?** Import the new `.mcaddon` and it replaces the old packs. Your grimoire, level, fruit, stars and squad are kept.
* Start with **Getting started** in the [documentation](https://github.com/priyanshchan/black-Clover-anime-addon-for-minecraft-bedrock-edition/blob/ccr-62b3f23b-74ycbu/docs/getting-started.md). In game, craft the **Magic Knight's Handbook** (a book and a mana crystal).

## New in v1.0.0: a living world

World events no longer appear in front of you. They are **incidents** at real places in the world.

* **Out of sight.** Every 3-6 minutes something happens about **60-200 blocks away**. You hear about it **by direction and distance**: *"A crimson light splits the sky - to the north-east, about 180 blocks away"*, with a horn or a gong from that direction.
* **Signs on the horizon.** Columns of smoke and pillars of light show where each incident is.
* **Track it.** The newest incident is tracked on your HUD: an arrow, its distance and its state (*gate strength 4/10, erupts in 6 min*). The Handbook's new **World events** page lists everything happening out there, nearest first, and tracks whichever you pick.
* **It develops while you are away.** Devil Gates grow stronger every minute. Rituals near completion. Hives lay eggs. Two armies wear each other down. A wounded knight bleeds out. A bandit raid marches on your home. Caravans and patrols cross the land.
* **Left alone, it plays out - with consequences.**
  * Gates erupt: the Devil Host grows stronger, and the Archdevil roams or ruins are left.
  * Rituals wake the Grimoire Devourer, and hives grow a queen.
  * Raids sack the land, and besieged camps are overrun.
  * Unwatched battles shift the factions' power, and knights fall.

  It all goes into the news (State of the Kingdom) and the event log.
* **It becomes real when you arrive.** An incident's units only appear when you get within about 50 blocks, in whatever state it has reached: a gate left open for eight minutes pours out ten devils, and a late arrival finds fewer soldiers. Walk away for a minute and it fades back into the simulation, remembering its survivors.
* **Hunters come from out of sight.** Thieves, Shadow Assassins, Tidewatch bounty hunters, Rogue Mage ambushes, rival squads and crimson-night devils come from behind you, 20-34 blocks away, with a warning.
* **New incidents:**
  * **Merchant caravans**: half the time they're ambushed, and saving one pays 60 doubloons.
  * **Mana springs**: stand in the geyser to refill your mana; crystals spill out.
  * **Magic Knight camps under siege.**
  * **Refugees** fleeing a Devil Gate, who point you to it.
  * **Ruins** left by failures, with salvage and the raiders still guarding it.
  * The giant bosses and a dominant faction's offensives happen out in the world too.

### Four new grimoires (24 spells)
* **Storm** - Storm Bolt, Squall Ring, Thunderhead, Tempest Eye, Lightning Lattice and the awakened **Heaven's Wrath**.
* **Blossom** - Petal Darts, Sakura Veil, Petal Storm, Thousand Blossoms, Blooming Prison and **Spring Eternal**.
* **Coral** - Coral Shard, Reef Spikes, Tide Pool, Barrier Reef, Riptide Reef and **Leviathan Reef**.
* **Dream** - Drowsy Mote, Lullaby, Nightmare, Dreamscape, Waking Nightmare and **Eternal Slumber**.

### Four new Abyss Fruits (16 powers)
* **Spark Fruit** - lightning in your veins (Speed). Spark Jolt, Thunder Step, Storm Cage, Gigavolt.
* **Echo Fruit** - you hear everything (Night Vision). Sonic Shot, Echo Burst, Resonance, Requiem Wave.
* **Sand Fruit** - a body of sand (tougher on dry ground). Sand Blades, Quicksand, Desert Coffin, Sandstorm.
* **Gale Fruit** - the wind carries you (higher jumps, gentle falls). Air Bullet, Tailwind, Cyclone, Sky Splitter.

### Mana Waygates
Build a network of rune arches (stone bricks, a mana crystal, an ender pearl and a rune shard; the merchant sells them too). Travel to any other Waygate for **20 mana + 15 per 100 blocks**, or a Mana Crystal when you are low. Name your gates. Every player shares the network.

### Also new
* Feats: **Answer the Call** (win 10 world events), **Gatebreaker** (seal 5 Devil Gates), **Wayfarer** (10 Waygate trips), **Late Bloomer** (receive one of the new grimoires).
* Missions: *win a world event where it happens*, *break up a bandit band*.

## Everything else since v0.1.0

### Spells that look the part (v0.9.0)
* Every spell and fruit power is redrawn in layers:
  * A magic circle forms, and the spell bursts out with a flash, a shock ring and sparks.
  * It flies with a glowing halo and an element trail.
  * It lands with a white-hot flash, its element's particles and smoke, and on the ground a shock ring, a dust wave and cracks.
* 48 new high-resolution particle effects, including bouncing sparks, solid beams with a bright core, real forked lightning, 8-frame fire and smoke, tumbling rocks, shards, leaves, snowflakes and rune glyphs.
* A rebuilt spell projectile (ringed orbs, crystal shards, crescent blades, spiral spears and **burning comets** for meteors).
* **Full-body cast animations** with wind-up, strike and follow-through: thrust, push, channel, call the sky, slam, sweep, spin, summon, power-up, heal and dash, plus a punch, stomp and roar for fruit users. Knights and companions use them too.

### Armour (v0.8.0)
* **Eight 3D armour sets** (32 pieces), each with bonuses for two and four pieces:
  * **Mana-Steel** and **Royal Knight**;
  * **Devilhide** and **Abyssal Scale** (the sea no longer drains fruit users);
  * **Shadowweave** (dodge, vanish and strike twice) and **Hive Chitin**;
  * **Runeplate** and **Archmage**.
* Materials drop from the foes that suit them: Devil Hide, Shadow Silk, Chitin Plates, Rune Shards, Arcane Thread, Leviathan Scales and craftable Mana-Steel Ingots.
* The **Magic Forge**:
  * forge any piece and **reinforce** it to +5;
  * **inscribe runes** (Vitality, Warding, Mana, Swiftness, Thorns, Focus);
  * sew a squad robe on as a **mantle** so you keep its perk;
  * salvage pieces for materials.
* Companions wear the armour you give them.

### Lairs, elites, potions and feats (v0.7.5)
* **Lairs** to raid: bandit hideouts (sometimes the Bandit King is home), hive nests and cultist shrines.
* **Elite enemies** with seven traits: Vampiric, Shielded, Swift, Explosive, Summoner, Arcane and Frenzied.
* Mana Potions, Elixirs of Iron and Haste, Smoke Bombs and **spell scrolls** (cast one spell from any grimoire).
* **Feats and titles** shown under your name.

### Foes that fight together (v0.7.0)
* **New enemies:**
  * the **Bandit Brotherhood**, whose thieves steal your doubloons and run;
  * **Shadow Assassins**;
  * **Cultists**, who summon imps;
  * **the Hive**: drones, and eggs that hatch.
* **Three bosses:** **the Bandit King**, **the Grimoire Devourer** (it eats your spells and throws them back) and **the Hive Queen**.
* **Warbands:** enemies focus the weakest target, surround it, call for help and break and run. **Bosses adapt** to how you fight.

### A grimoire and a fruit (v0.6.0)
* Every companion fights with a grimoire **and** an Abyss Fruit.
* Every grimoire gained a **level-12 move**, plus Black Hurricane for Anti-Magic.
* Every fruit gained a **fourth power**, which unlocks at Will 2.

### The companion update (v0.5.0)
* **Personalities** and **specialisations**: Vanguard, Arcanist, Warden and Ranger.
* **Party tactics**, combos and marks, danger-aware positioning, and **revives**.
* Mood, **camps with a night watch**, talking, and **personal trials**.
* A **reserve** of six more knights you can send on **expeditions**.
* A Training Dummy, sparring and **rival squads**.
* Companions fly brooms with you.
* Four grimoires: Gravity, Moon, Thorn and Ash.
* The **Soul Puppeteer**, a boss that takes control of your companions.

### Brooms, missions and new foes (v0.3.0)
* **Magic brooms** that fly on your mana.
* **Daily missions** and the Mission Board.
* Five **amulets** for your off hand.
* An **awakened** spell at level 15 for every grimoire.
* **Rogue Mages**.
* **The Witch Sisters** twin boss and **the Bone Dragon**.
* **Magic Knight camps**.

### Companions and smarter enemies (v0.2.0)
* A saved **companion party** with the Companion Whistle, orders, stances, formations, bond and Union Magic.
* Smarter enemies, and bosses with threat tables, three phases, desperation moves, telegraphs and an enrage timer.
* **The Fallen Captain**.
* Eight grimoires (Cotton, Paper, Smoke, Glass, Gold, Beast, Spirit, Rune).
* Eight Abyss Fruits, including the Lion, Shark, Phoenix and Spider beast forms.

## Known issues
* **Balance is still a first pass**, so damage, boss health and costs may change. Please report anything too strong or too weak.
* Incidents become real when you get within about 50 blocks. If your world's **simulation distance** is lower than 4 chunks, you may need to get closer.
* Horizon signs are particles, so very low render or particle settings can hide them. The HUD arrow always points the way.
* Very large battles can be heavy on low-end devices. A per-tick particle budget drops the extra effect layers to keep things smooth.
* The Stone Colossus can get stuck on rough terrain, and the Phantom Galleon is steered by script and may drift.

Found a bug or a balance problem? Please [open an issue](https://github.com/priyanshchan/black-Clover-anime-addon-for-minecraft-bedrock-edition/issues/new/choose).
