## v1.2.0 – Dragonbound & Kingdoms

**Black Clover: Grimoire Awakening** is an unofficial Black Clover fan addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. v1.2.0 adds three things:
* **A Mana Wyvern of your own** to hatch, raise and ride.
* **The neighbouring kingdoms** holding the Marches.
* **The Royal Capital**, a walled town near home.

### Download and install

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| **`BlackClover_GrimoireAwakening_v1.2.0.mcaddon`** | **Start here.** Open it and Minecraft imports both packs. |
| `BlackClover_BP_v1.2.0.mcpack` | the behavior pack on its own |
| `BlackClover_RP_v1.2.0.mcpack` | the resource pack on its own |

* Needs **Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer**. No experimental toggles.
* **Upgrading?** Import the new `.mcaddon` and it replaces the old packs. Everything you have is kept.
  * The Capital rises the next time you come near its site.
  * Regions you haven't discovered yet in the Marches get their kingdom and its forts.
* Everything is explained on the [Dragonbound & Kingdoms](https://github.com/priyanshchan/black-Clover-anime-addon-for-minecraft-bedrock-edition/blob/main/docs/dragonbound.md) page.

## A wyvern of your own

**Hatching**
* **Eggs**: Wyvern Eggs come from roost hoards and from slain wild wyverns.
* **Keep it in its element**:
  * **Ember**: fire, a campfire, lava or magma.
  * **Frost**: snow or ice.
  * **Storm**: a lightning rod, copper, or high ground.
* After four minutes it **hatches, bonded to you**.

**Growing up**
* It **grows** while you are near: hatchling, then juvenile, then adult. Feed it meat, fish or mana crystals to hurry it along.
* A **juvenile fights beside you**, breathing its element at monsters.
* An **adult can be saddled**.

**Riding**
* **Controls**: look to steer, hold forward to fly, jump to climb, sneak to step off.
* **Breath**: use the **Wyvern Whistle** while riding to breathe fire, frost or lightning where you look.
* **On the ground**, the whistle switches follow / stay / roam, and sneak-using it calls your wyvern to your side.
* If your wyvern falls, it **leaves an egg behind**.

## The kingdoms of the Marches

Past the Forsaken Realm, the land belongs to the neighbouring kingdoms.

* **The Diamond Kingdom (west)**
  * Its soldiers are crystal-armoured **Legionnaires** fighting with crystal and steel magic.
  * **Patrols** find you in its Marches.
  * Its **Diamond Border Forts** are held by a **Diamond Commander**.
* **The Spade Kingdom (north)**
  * Its soldiers are hooded **Zealots** fighting with dark and gravity magic.
  * **Patrols** find you in its Marches.
  * Its **Spade Citadels** are held by a **Spade Inquisitor**.
* **The Heart Kingdom (east)** is a friend.
  * Its **Guardians** fight monsters beside you.
  * Each **Heart Sanctum** has a priestess who trades spirit relics and **Spirit Draughts**.
* **Spoils of war**: Diamond Shards and Spade Sigils sell to the Capital's quartermaster for doubloons, plus a star for every ten.
* **Kingdom relics**: three new ones, the **Legion Prism**, the **Zealot's Brand** and the **Spirit Blossom**.

## The Royal Capital

Once per world, a walled town rises 150-230 blocks from spawn. It has:
* walls, towers and a gate;
* a plaza with a fountain and a **Mana Waygate**;
* the **Magic Knight HQ**, with a Mission Board, a War Table and a Squad Banner;
* a smithy with a **Magic Forge**, and an inn;
* a market of shopkeepers, including the quartermaster;
* Magic Knights on guard.

**No monsters spawn and no ambushes come** inside its walls, and your first visit makes it **your home**.

## Also new

* Seven new feats:
  * Hatchling, Wyvern Bond and Dragon Rider;
  * Border Warden, Fort Breaker and Friend of Hearts;
  * To the Capital.
* A new mission: *Hold the border*.
* Wyvern Saddles are now sold by the merchant.
* The Atlas shows the way to the Capital and how your wyverns are growing.
* Feat progress is now saved every couple of seconds.

## Known issues

* **Balance is a first pass.** Wyvern growth time, riding speed, fort difficulty and spoils prices may change - please report anything off.
* **Riding is steered by script.** Flying near the ground or through narrow gaps can be bumpy.
* **The Capital's site.** It picks the flattest ground it can find nearby, so on mountainous worlds it may sit on a raised foundation.

Found a bug or a balance problem? Please [open an issue](https://github.com/priyanshchan/black-Clover-anime-addon-for-minecraft-bedrock-edition/issues/new/choose).
