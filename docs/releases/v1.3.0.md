## v1.3.0 – Devil Union

**Black Clover: Grimoire Awakening** is an unofficial Black Clover fan addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. v1.3.0 brings the devils of the underworld into the world:
* **Devil hosts** that change as their union with the devil deepens.
* **Possessed Magic Knights** to save.
* **A pact with a devil of your own**: join with it at 50% and 100% union.
* **The Crowned Vessel**, host of a higher-rank devil.

### Download and install

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| **`BlackClover_GrimoireAwakening_v1.3.0.mcaddon`** | **Start here.** Open it and Minecraft imports both packs. |
| `BlackClover_BP_v1.3.0.mcpack` | the behavior pack on its own |
| `BlackClover_RP_v1.3.0.mcpack` | the resource pack on its own |

* Needs **Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer**. No experimental toggles.
* **Upgrading?** Import the new `.mcaddon` and it replaces the old packs. Everything you have is kept.
  * Regions you haven't discovered yet can hold an Underworld Breach.
* Everything is explained on the [Devil Union](https://github.com/priyanshchan/black-Clover-anime-addon-for-minecraft-bedrock-edition/blob/main/docs/devil-union.md) page, and in-game in the Handbook (**Devil Union**).

## Devil hosts

A devil cannot walk this world without a body - so it borrows one.

* **Four devils** walk in hosts, each with its own look and magic:
  * **Vharog**, the Cinder Maw - hellfire.
  * **Sileth**, the Pale Choir - grave frost.
  * **Morvane**, the Hollow Crown - crushing gravity.
  * **Ghorza**, the Red Hunger - blood that heals its host.
* **Union**: hurt a host and its devil digs in.
  * **50% union**: horns, a devil's arm and glowing marks break through. It heals and grows stronger.
  * **100% union**: wings and a tail. It flies at you and casts its devil's greatest spell.
* **Where**: the Forsaken Realm and the Marches at night, Devil-Scarred regions, and with Spade patrols.
* **Spoils**: only a host beaten **at full union** leaves its devil's **Pact Sigil** behind (and a Host Horn).

## Possession

At night a lesser devil may **seize a Magic Knight** near you. Beat it below about a third of its health and the devil is cast out - the knight is free. **A killing blow kills the knight too.**

## A pact of your own

* **The offer**: use a Pact Sigil to hear its devil - its **boon** and its **price**, always on while you are bound.
* **The union gauge** fills as you fight.
* **50% union**: use the sigil to join. Horns, stronger spells and blows, and two of the devil's moves (use: cast, sneak-use: switch).
* **100% union**: with a full gauge and **bond 3**. Wings to glide and leap, far greater power, and the devil's third, greatest move.
* **The devil's hold**: while you are joined, your devil pushes to take over. Let its hold fill and it **takes over** for ten seconds, lashing out at everything near you - friends too. A **Binding Chain** holds it back; your bond and Will push it away.

## The Crowned Vessel

A knight-captain who gave himself to **Vaelgrim, the Crowned Abyss**, a higher-rank devil.
* It waits in **Underworld Breaches** in the dangerous lands, or answers an **Abyssal Crown** raised at night.
* Its union deepens as you fight: horns through its crown at half health, wings and a burning halo at a quarter.
* Beat it for **Vaelgrim's own Pact Sigil**.

## Also new

* Seven new feats:
  * Pact-Bound, Half a Devil, Full Union, Devil's Equal;
  * Exorcist, Host Hunter, Crown-Breaker.
* A new omen, **The Underworld Stirs**, and a new mission, *Cast out the devils*.
* Binding Chains at the merchant; a Devil Union page in the Handbook; your devil in the Atlas.

## Known issues

* **Balance is a first pass.** Union gauge rates, the devil's hold, host strength and the Vessel may change - please report anything off.
* **Your union shows as particles.** The horns and wings around you are drawn with particles, so they can trail a little when you move fast.
