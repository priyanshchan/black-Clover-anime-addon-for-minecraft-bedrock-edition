## v1.1.0 – the Untamed Kingdom

**Black Clover: Grimoire Awakening** is an unofficial Black Clover fan addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. v1.1.0 is about **world-building and randomness**: the overworld becomes a kingdom of named regions, each with its own danger and character, with landmarks to find over every hill, random loot worth finding, tougher elites, and a little chaos every day.

### Download and install

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| **`BlackClover_GrimoireAwakening_v1.1.0.mcaddon`** | **Start here.** Open it and Minecraft imports both packs. |
| `BlackClover_BP_v1.1.0.mcpack` | the behavior pack on its own |
| `BlackClover_RP_v1.1.0.mcpack` | the resource pack on its own |

* Needs **Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer**. No experimental toggles.
* **Upgrading from v1.0.0?** Import the new `.mcaddon` and it replaces the old packs. Your grimoire, level, fruit, stars, squad, armour and Waygates are kept. Regions and landmarks appear as you explore - in old worlds too.
* Everything new is explained on [The Untamed Kingdom](https://github.com/priyanshchan/black-Clover-anime-addon-for-minecraft-bedrock-edition/blob/main/docs/exploration.md) page, and in game in the Handbook's new **Atlas**.

## A world with names

* The overworld is cut into **320-block regions**, and every one is a place of its own: *Kelmoor Fen*, *the Peaks of Varowbarrow*, *the Whispering Pines*. Its name comes from its land.
* Each region also has:
  * **A realm**, set by its distance from home: the **Noble Realm**, the **Common Realm**, the **Forsaken Realm**, then **the Marches** toward the neighbouring kingdoms.
  * **Danger stars** from one to five.
  * **One to three traits.**
* Walk into a new region and its name, realm and danger appear on your screen, with a line of lore.
* **Everything comes from your world's seed.** The same world always tells the same story; a new world tells a new one.

**The twelve traits:**
* **Mana-Rich** (+30% mana regeneration) and **Ley Line** (+10% spell power).
* **Wild Magic**: your spells may surge (cost nothing, or cast twice) or misfire.
* **Haunted**: the Hollow walk at night and the slain rise as wraiths.
* **Bandit Country**: ambushes on the road.
* **Devil-Scarred**: imps, and more Devil Gates.
* **Wyvern Territory**: roosts, and wyverns overhead.
* **Fae Wilds**: sprites by day, Will-o'-Wisps by night.
* **Ancient Ruins**: more spires and crypts.
* **Sanctuary**: quiet, and full of wanderers.
* **Hoard-Rich**: maps and caches.
* **Untamed**: twice the elites.

## Something over every hill

Every region has **three to eight landmarks**. They are raised out of sight as you come near, fitted to the ground and filled with loot for the region's danger. Their guards wake as you approach.

* **Mage Spires**: five floors, each with its guards and its chest, and a spiral stair up the middle. The **Spire Warden** waits on the roof, and its treasure appears there when it falls.
* **Wyvern Roosts**: an **Ember, Frost or Storm Mana Wyvern** sleeps on a hoard of gold and bone. Wake it and it hunts you from the air with elemental breath, dives, tail sweeps, wing gusts and a storm of its element.
* **Ancient Catacombs**: a stair down into five to ten rooms, **laid out differently every time**. Expect:
  * crypts, libraries and treasure rooms;
  * spawner halls and flame-trap rooms;
  * prisons with a captive Magic Knight (free them and they may join your squad);
  * shrines;
  * the Guardian's hall at the far end.
* **Ruined hamlets**: some empty, some held by bandits, some haunted, some with a hermit living there.
* **Old graveyards** and **forgotten battlefields**: the **Hollow** claw out of the graves at night.
* **Mana Obelisks**: pray for a boon, or risk a curse.
* **Fairy rings**: drop an item in and the sprites trade it for a random gift.
* **More landmarks**:
  * ruined watchtowers;
  * wrecked caravans (sometimes bait);
  * star craters;
  * colossal bones with treasure in the skull;
  * **Lost Waygates**, which join your network;
  * hedge witch huts.
* **Mana Spawners** keep calling foes until you break them, and about one chest in eight is a **Mimic**.

## Random loot

* Rarities run from **Common to Mythic**, and the more dangerous the land, the better the rolls.
* **Relics** - the Signet Ring, Rune Talisman, Bone Idol, Crystal Eye, Wyvern Fang and Spirit Feather - roll random affixes. Up to three work at once from your hotbar. They can give:
  * max mana, mana regeneration, spell power or shorter cooldowns;
  * health, damage cuts, thorns, life drain or melee damage;
  * luck, magic XP or doubloons;
  * Swiftness, Jump Boost, Night Vision, Water Breathing or Fire Resistance.
* **Rolled weapons** (swords and axes, stone to netherite) come with procs: extra damage, burning, freezing, lightning, life drain, mana drain, executions, giant-felling, cleaving and Haste.
* Legendary and Mythic items have **names of their own**. Not happy with a roll? **Reforge** it at the Magic Forge.

## Champions and Infernals

* Elites now come in three tiers: **Elite** (one trait), **Champion** (two) and **Infernal** (three).
* Ten new traits: **Blinking, Frostbound, Stormcaller, Gravitic, Regenerating, Reflective, Webbing, Splitting, Ghostly and Juggernaut**.
* They get commoner the farther you roam. Infernals always carry a rolled treasure.

## Encounters, omens and wanderers

* **Your own actions wake things:**
  * chop a tree and a **Rootwarden** may tear free;
  * dig deep and a wraith seeps out of the stone;
  * cut grass and a **Mana Sprite** pops up - it will steal from your pack, so catch it;
  * sleep at night and you may wake to a **Hollow Knight**.
* **Will-o'-Wisps** lead you to hidden caches, or to traps.
* **Omens** colour each day:
  * Mana Tide, Wild Surge and Lucky Stars;
  * **Starfall** - stars land nearby, each leaving a Star Fragment;
  * Hollow Night, Wyvern Migration and Bandit Moon;
  * Merchants' Fair, Fae Revel and Calm Skies.
* **Wanderers** walk the roads: pilgrims, cartographers, peddlers, hermit mages, bards and lost knights. Each has a name, rumours that point at real landmarks, odd things to trade, and favours to ask.

## Also new

* **Explorer's Maps** (lead to a landmark nobody has found).
* **Torn Journal Pages** (written from the land around you; some mark a buried cache).
* The **Spire Core** (raises a Mana Waygate on the spot).
* **Star Fragments**, **Sprite Dust**, **Mimic Teeth** (the merchant buys them) and **Hollow Embers**.
* **Wyvernscale armour**, a ninth set forged from Wyvern Scales.
* The Handbook's **Atlas**: your region, today's omen, the regions and landmarks you have found, your relics at work, and an arrow to the nearest unknown landmark.
* Nine new feats, three new missions, and new cheats: `/scriptevent bc:region`, `bc:landmark [kind]`, `bc:omen [id]` and `bc:wanderer [kind]`.

## Known issues

* **Balance is a first pass.** Wyvern and Warden health, elite odds and loot rarity may change - please report anything too strong or too weak.
* **Landmarks need flat-ish ground.** A landmark that finds no room on steep ground or water is skipped for good.
* **They can appear in view.** They are raised out of sight where possible, but walking straight at an unbuilt landmark from far away can show it appearing in the distance.
* **Region names read the land where you first enter**, so a region that is half forest and half plains is named after the half you came in from.

Found a bug or a balance problem? Please [open an issue](https://github.com/priyanshchan/black-Clover-anime-addon-for-minecraft-bedrock-edition/issues/new/choose).
